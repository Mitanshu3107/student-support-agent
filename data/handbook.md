# Student Policy Handbook (PGDM 2025-27)

This handbook is a sample policy document for the Student Support Agent. All amounts are in Indian Rupees (Rs).

## 1. Fee Payment Schedule

The PGDM programme fee is payable in three equal instalments. Instalment 1 is due on 15 July 2026, instalment 2 on 5 November 2026, and instalment 3 on 15 February 2027. Fees can be paid through the online payment portal, by demand draft, or at the accounts office counter. A payment is considered complete only when the receipt is generated by the accounts office.

## 2. Late Payment and Late Fee

If an instalment is not paid by its due date, a late fee of Rs 500 per working day applies, up to a maximum of Rs 10,000 per instalment. Students with an overdue instalment for more than 15 working days may be barred from term-end examinations and from accessing the placement portal until the dues are cleared.

## 3. Payment Portal Failure

If the online payment portal fails or the transaction is declined on or before the due date, the student must raise a support request with the accounts office and attach the transaction reference number or a screenshot of the failure. If the student raises the request within one working day of the due date, the late fee is waived for up to two working days while the issue is investigated. Payment made on the next working day after a documented portal failure will not attract a late fee. Without a documented portal failure, the standard late fee in section 2 applies. If money was debited but the payment was not recorded, the amount is refunded or adjusted within 7 working days.

## 4. Scholarships

The institute offers a Merit Scholarship and a Need-Based Scholarship. The Merit Scholarship ranges from 10 to 50 percent of the tuition fee and is awarded based on admission test score and academic performance. The Need-Based Scholarship is awarded on the basis of documented family income and is reviewed by the scholarship committee. Applications are reviewed within 15 working days of receipt.

## 5. Scholarship Renewal

A scholarship is renewed for the next academic year only if the student maintains a CGPA of at least 3.4 and attendance of at least 80 percent. A student who falls below either threshold is placed on a one-term probation, and the scholarship is withdrawn if the thresholds are not met at the end of the probation term.

## 6. Course Changes and Electives

Students may change an elective within the first two weeks of the term by submitting a course change request to the academic office. Core courses cannot be changed. A change request is approved subject to seat availability and programme-level prerequisites. After the two-week window, a course change requires approval from the Programme Director and a fee of Rs 1,000.

## 7. Attendance Requirement

A minimum of 80 percent attendance is required in each course to be eligible for the term-end examination. Students with attendance between 70 and 80 percent may apply for condonation with a valid medical or other documented reason. Attendance below 70 percent is not condoned.

## 8. Refund Policy

Fee refund requests made within 15 days of the start of the programme are refunded in full after deducting Rs 25,000 as an administrative charge. No refund is available after 15 days from the start of the programme, except for duplicate or erroneous payments, which are refunded in full.

## 9. Support Requests

Students can raise support requests for fee issues, scholarships, academic queries and technical problems. Each request is assigned a ticket number and responded to within 2 working days. Urgent fee-related requests are responded to within 1 working day.
